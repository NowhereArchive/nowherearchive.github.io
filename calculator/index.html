Attack Speed Breakpoints

The frame rate of normal attacks in battles is 15. This means:

  • If your attack speed is 1.0, one attack takes 15 frames
  • If your attack speed is 15.0, one attack takes 1 frame
  • Higher speed = fewer frames per attack

The problem: Attack speed buffs only matter if they push you to a faster frame tier. In between breakpoints, bonuses do nothing.

Example: Your sinner has attack speed 1.01 (which takes 15 frames). You want to add a Crimebrand buff. Would a 5% buff help?

Calculation: 1.01 × 1.05 = 1.0605

According to the table below, to reach 14 frames you need speed ≥ 1.07142. Your new speed (1.0605) is still below that threshold, so the buff does nothing, you're still at 15 frames.

But with a 10% buff:1.01 × 1.10 = 1.111

Now 1.111 is above 1.07142, so you break into 14 frames.
